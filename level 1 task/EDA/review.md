**1.Food data** 

Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis (EDA) of McDonald's menu dataset provided valuable insights into the nutritional composition and calorie distribution of its offerings. Key findings include:

Caloric Content: High-calorie items are prevalent, especially in categories like burgers and desserts, emphasizing the importance of portion control.

Nutritional Imbalance: Many items exceed daily recommended levels of sodium, sugar, and fat, suggesting limited options for health-conscious consumers.

Category Comparison: Beverages and sides often have lower caloric density compared to main courses.

Healthy Options: Salads and lighter meals cater to health-focused consumers but make up a small portion of the menu.

These insights can help McDonald's target menu optimization and personalized marketing strategies to align with consumer preferences and health trends.


Recommendations based on EDA

Consider offering lower-calorie options in high-calorie categories.

Reduce items with high total and saturated fat to attract health-conscious customers.

Promote items with high protein and dietary fiber, which appeal to fitness-oriented customers.

Monitor nutritional values, especially for sodium and cholesterol, as excessive values may deter health-focused customers.
